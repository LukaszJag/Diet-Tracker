7 TERMS FOR URGENT, LONG-TERM TASKS 
(Increasing in scale and time)

1. Priority Deliverable
- Timeframe: Days to Weeks
- What it is: A large, urgent task that requires intense focus and cannot be done in a single sitting. It is a "must-do" that requires you to block out significant chunks of your immediate schedule.
- Example: Writing a 50-page business proposal for a major client whose deadline is next week, or studying for mid-term exams.

2. Mission-Critical Sprint
- Timeframe: Weeks to 2 Months
- What it is: A vital phase of work where dropping the ball means failure. A sprint implies moving fast with urgency, but a mission-critical sprint requires sustaining that exhausting, urgent pace for a month or two to cross a specific finish line.
- Example: Rebuilding a broken, revenue-generating website, or doing a 6-week intensive coding bootcamp to get certified.

3. High-Stakes Campaign
- Timeframe: 3 to 6 Months
- What it is: A series of coordinated, urgent sprints bound together by a massive looming deadline. You can't just "sprint" the whole time or you will burn out; you have to treat it like a military or marketing campaign—urgently hitting weekly milestones to ensure the final half-year target is met.
- Example: Launching a brand-new product line for a business, organizing a massive international conference, or running a local political campaign.

4. Marathon Project
- Timeframe: 6 Months to 1 Year
- What it is: A heavy, overarching to-do item that requires constant, weekly progress or you will fall behind. It requires the pacing and endurance of a marathon, but the underlying stakes keep the urgency high all year long.
- Example: Writing and editing a full novel, building a house from the ground up, or physically training for an Ironman triathlon.

5. Strategic Initiative
- Timeframe: 1 to 3 Years
- What it is: A high-priority, multi-year goal. The "urgency" here isn't a tight deadline for tomorrow; rather, the urgency is that your future success depends on starting it now and refusing to lose momentum for years.
- Example: Earning a master's degree, or pivoting a company's entire business model to keep up with new technology (like shifting an entire company to AI).

6. Monumental Undertaking
- Timeframe: 5 to 10 Years (A Decade-Level Goal)
- What it is: A massive, transformative goal that dictates a whole era of your life or an organization's history. It is highly urgent because massive resources are mobilized and the stakes are life-changing, but it takes nearly a decade to fully realize.
- Example: Working your way from an entry-level job to making Partner at a law firm, going through medical school and residency to become a surgeon, or a space agency engineering and launching a rover to Mars.

7. Magnum Opus / Grand Challenge
- Timeframe: Decades to a Lifetime
- What it is: The highest possible level of a long-term task. "Magnum Opus" translates to "Great Work." This is your ultimate, burning priority. It is always urgent because it defines your life's purpose or an organization's legacy, taking immense dedication to fulfill. 
- Example: Raising a child, working to cure a complex disease, fighting global climate change, or building a multi-generational family business.

--------------------------------------------------
SUMMARY OF THE PROGRESSION:
Priority Deliverable -> Mission-Critical Sprint -> High-Stakes Campaign -> Marathon Project -> Strategic Initiative -> Monumental Undertaking -> Magnum Opus.
